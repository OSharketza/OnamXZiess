The trust question

Every serious worry about AI in your practice — answered.

Putting AI near your patients raises fair questions: safety, control, the edge cases, and whether it fits the systems you already run. Here's how VisioGen answers each — with a ZEISS-trained optometrist behind every reply.

Surgeon & owner
Hallucinations & wrong answers
"What if it makes something up and says the wrong thing under my name?"

It's scoped to eye care and grounded in your own approved answers, so it doesn't improvise. And a ZEISS-trained optometrist approves every reply before it sends. It never diagnoses.

Clinical lead & optometrists
Edge cases & red flags
"What about the urgent cases — the ones a bot should never touch?"

Anything urgent or outside its lane is caught and escalated to your team, with an urgent-care nudge to the patient. The guardrails are built in, not an optional setting.

Practice directors & admins
Control & the daily schedule
"Do I stay in control of what it says — and does it fit how we run the clinic?"

You set the voice and the boundaries, and you see every conversation. It works around your hours and OD schedule, and hands clean, ready-to-book leads to your front desk.

IT & operations
Your patient systems
"Will it actually work with our patient-management and EHR systems?"

It connects to the tools you already run — like ModMed / EMA and Epic Kaleidoscope — with a roughly 30-minute setup and nothing to rip out.

One reason all of them hold: a ZEISS-trained optometrist approves every reply before it ever reaches a patient.
